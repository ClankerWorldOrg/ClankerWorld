# Tool upgrades and carrying limits

- [ ] In a normal Windows game, let a founder without a basket or sack make and use a stone axe. Check that they can gather wood and a tree seed, then deliver wood to their Blacksmith without repeatedly reporting a full load.
- [ ] Give a founder a usable basket or sack and a stone or iron axe. Check that gathering still produces the larger tool harvest when the whole load fits, and that saving and loading during delivery keeps their goods and progress.
