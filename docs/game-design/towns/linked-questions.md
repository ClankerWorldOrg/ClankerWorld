# Cross-system questions

Open questions that connect these systems.

## Questions linking these systems

These remain open; they are not new decisions.

- **Later law and property interactions.** Adoption, law scope, land hearings
   and initial nonviolent enforcement are agreed in [Laws and enforcement](laws-and-enforcement.md). Agent-created laws
   remain breakable while the simulation protects physical facts and validated
   ownership changes. Findings between Towns, Town inheritance law and the
   stages of theft and stronger enforcement were agreed on October 8. The
   detailed designs of crime and physical enforcement, including physical
   responses to illegal occupation, still wait for their stages.

- **Homes, membership and family growth.** The [membership
   rules](households.md#household-membership) settle one household at a time, unanimous
   adult admission, voluntary adult departure and solo formation. The
   [resident-capacity rules](households.md#house-resident-capacity-and-relocation) settle
   footprint-scaled places, family priority, births and relocation. The [goods
   and departure rules](households.md#household-goods-and-departure) settle personal
   ownership, collection access, the food allowance and care during a living
   adult's departure. [Town membership](town-membership.md#town-membership) settles recorded
   affiliation, homeless residents, ordinary newcomer approval and dependent
   children. [Borders, abandonment and salvage](town-membership.md#borders-abandoned-towns-and-salvage)
   settle the empty-Town exception, communal access and founding new Towns,
   and the goods and departure rules settle an empty household's property.
   Cross-Town dependent moves still follow admission rules in issue
   [#602](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/602); remaining
   border assignment or dispute cases stay open.

- **Building storage catalogue.** Houses start at 1×1 and may expand to
    1×2 or 2×2, Warehouses 2×2 → 2×3, and Stores may be 1×1 or 1×2. These
    choices raise storage and House resident capacity under the agreed
    footprint table in [House resident capacity and relocation](households.md#house-resident-capacity-and-relocation). The other chosen building
    footprints are in the accepted roster in [Agreed content and building sizes](buildings-and-projects.md#agreed-content-and-building-sizes). Storage sizes and expansion
    costs are agreed as provisional values in [Buildings, land, Towns, and
    animals](buildings-and-animals.md#buildings-land-towns-and-animals). Decide other construction
    costs and expansion triggers.
